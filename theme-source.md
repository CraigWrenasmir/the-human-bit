# Universal Sandpit theme reference

Reference: https://theuniversalsandpit.org/ — inspected 29 September 2026.

The player and film use the current homepage’s authored design language:

- Background: `#f5f4fb`.
- Ink: `#2a2a3a`; secondary text: `#5a5a6a`.
- Lavender: `#9090c0`; deep lavender: `#6b6b9d`.
- Accent colours: red `#d64a4a`, yellow `#e8c547`, green `#5ab97a`, blue `#5a7ec2`.
- Space Mono headings and labels; Inter body and speech text.
- Soft white cards, lavender borders and shadows, rounded page controls, `//` labels and a block cursor motif.

The pixel characters use the established friendly design. In version 07, Craig is mirrored to face Josh and has blue eye accents; the title is The Human Bit. The film’s stepped speech balloon remains part of the established character design. Version 06 removes the lighter panels behind the characters so the lavender background continues seamlessly around them; the page retains its white cards.
